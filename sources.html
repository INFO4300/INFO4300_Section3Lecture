Learning and media design

Brame, C. J. (2016). Effective educational videos: Principles and guidelines for maximizing student learning from video content. CBE-Life Sciences Education, 15(4), es6. Article DOI. An accessible author guide describes the design principles.

This lesson uses learner-controlled segments, complementary visuals, signaling, conversational narration, and application with feedback. It makes no universal attention-span claim.

Accessibility references: W3C accessible audio and video guidance and WCAG 2.2 quick reference.

Use the lesson your way

  • Start the lesson, then use Back, Next, or chapter navigation. You may revisit or skip every activity.
  • Audio starts only after Play. Use Replay, native audio controls, the speed selector, or the narration volume and mute settings under Sound & display.
  • Captions follow the recorded narration. Each chapter has text and a visual description; the complete descriptive transcript includes every practice and game explanation.
  • Use untimed cards, pause, reduced motion, and optional game sounds. Number-key shortcuts are limited to game focus and can be turned off.
  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move among controls, and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Browser zoom and text-size settings are supported.

The design targets WCAG 2.2 AA. This is not a certification of full conformance. A screen-reader usability study has not been completed. If media fails, text and navigation remain available.
